# Home Page

## Purpose
Public landing page that explains the value and converts visitors to signups.

## Hero Section
- Headline: "Optimize your Google Play listing faster"
- Subheadline: "Track keywords, generate better metadata, understand competitors, and improve your app store visibility with less complexity."
- CTA: "Start free trial" → signup
- Secondary CTA: "See how it works" → demo video or features

## Features Section
- Keyword tracking: "See your keyword rankings and trends in one place."
- AI listing optimizer: "Generate titles, descriptions, and CTAs optimized for conversion."
- Competitor analysis: "Find gaps and opportunities compared to top apps."
- Review insights: "Turn reviews into actionable improvements."
- Localization: "Reach more users with market-specific suggestions."

## Social Proof Section

- "Improved keyword rankings by 25% in first month."
- "Saved hours writing better app descriptions."
- "Finally understand my competitors."

## Final CTA Section

- Primary CTA: "Get started free"
