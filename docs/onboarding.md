# Onboarding Flow

> **Canonical spec:** [`app-pages/onboarding.md`](../app-pages/onboarding.md)

## Step 1: Welcome
- "Welcome to PlayStore XYZ"
- "Let's set up your first workspace and app."
- CTA: "Continue"

## Step 2: Create Workspace
- Workspace name.
- Optional team members.
- "Workspaces let you manage multiple apps and teams."
- CTA: "Create workspace"

## Step 3: Add First App
- App name.
- Package name or Play Store URL.
- Target countries.
- "We'll start tracking your app's performance."
- CTA: "Add app"

## Step 4: Add Keywords
- "Add your first keywords to track."
- Quick suggestions based on app category.
- "Track up to 50 keywords on Starter."
- CTA: "Start tracking"

## Step 5: First Insight
- Show initial ASO score or keyword opportunities.
- "Here's what we found about your app."
- CTA: "Explore dashboard" → dashboard home

## Completion
- "You're all set!"
- "Your first keyword data will appear in 24 hours."
- Quick actions: "Optimize listing", "Add more keywords", "Invite team"
