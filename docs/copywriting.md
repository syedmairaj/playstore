# Copywriting

## Brand Voice
- Clear.
- Smart.
- Human.
- Direct.
- Calm.

## Tone Rules
- Avoid hype.
- Avoid jargon.
- Avoid enterprise buzzwords.
- Keep sentences short and useful.
- Make the product sound premium without sounding fake.

## Homepage Copy Direction
- Track better keywords.
- Improve your Google Play listing.
- Understand competitors faster.
- Turn reviews into actions.
- Localize with confidence.

## Button Copy
- Start tracking.
- Improve listing.
- Analyze competitor.
- View insights.
- Generate suggestions.

## Microcopy Style
- Friendly.
- Precise.
- Helpful.
- Short.
